About

Reza Skandari is Assistant Professor of Health Analytics and Operations at Imperial College Business School. Reza's research asks how data and analytical methods can improve decisions in healthcare, for patients, clinicians, healthcare managers and policymakers alike. The work develops methods in decision analytics, optimisation, machine learning and health economics, and applies them with clinical partners to chronic disease, cancer, organ transplantation and the organisation of care.

Reza is a member of the Centre for Health Economics & Policy Innovation (CHEPI). Before joining Imperial in 2018, Reza was a Senior Research Analyst in the Department of Medicine at the University of Chicago, and received a PhD from the Sauder School of Business at the University of British Columbia.

Research themes

Personalised and stratified care

Treatment plans that learn from each patient's data, and care designs that are tailored yet simple enough to implement.

Designing care for chronic disease and cancer

When and how to screen, monitor, treat and follow up patients with long-term conditions such as cancer, kidney disease and cystic fibrosis.

The organisation of care

Clinician workload, new care models and decision support, from primary care to heart transplantation.

The value of medical innovations

Cost-effectiveness of new medicines, devices, diagnostics and models of care, especially in diabetes.
