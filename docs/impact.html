Research in policy and practice

Examples of research that has been cited in clinical guidelines and policy, used in practice, recognised or covered beyond academia. Each item links to its source.

Guidelines and policy

  • Cost-effectiveness analysis of vascular access referral policies in chronic kidney disease, cited in the National Kidney Foundation KDOQI vascular access guideline updates.
  • Cost-effectiveness of MODY genetic testing, cited in a US insurer medical policy on MODY genetic testing.
  • Cost-effectiveness of continuous glucose monitoring (DIAMOND trial), cited by the Center for Health Care Strategies in payer resources on CGM reimbursement.

Practice

  • Cost-effectiveness of shared telemedicine appointments for young adults with type 1 diabetes (CoYoT1 trial) informed the CoYoT1 to California pilot.
  • The productivity requirements of medical scribe programmes, discussed by the American Medical Association.
  • The time needed to provide adult primary care, discussed by the American Academy of Family Physicians.

Recognition

  • 2025 · Best Student Paper Finalist, AMIA Annual Symposium (heart transplant waitlist mortality).
  • 2018 · Tufts CEVR Cost-Effectiveness Analysis Paper of the Year (individualised glycaemic control for US adults with type 2 diabetes).
  • 2012 · Lusted Award Finalist, Society for Medical Decision Making.
